## Power Your Adventures with Solar Energy

SolarSnap converts sunlight into electricity to charge compatible devices. When you're camping or exploring the outdoors and your device battery is running low, SolarSnap gives you a way to charge using sunlight.

## Designed for Life Outdoors

### 1. Foldable Design

Pack SolarSnap easily for your next trip. Its foldable design makes it convenient to carry when camping, hiking, or travelling.

### 2. USB Charging Outputs

Charge compatible devices using SolarSnap's USB outputs. Keep your essential devices powered while enjoying time outdoors.

### 3. Built-in Kickstand

Position the solar panel towards the sunlight with the built-in kickstand. Adjusting the panel helps you find a suitable position for capturing sunlight.
